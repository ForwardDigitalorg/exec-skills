# my-day

A short email each morning with what needs you today.

- **Before first use:** fill in your time zone and workday hours on your team's settings page.
- **Start it:** say "set up my day" once. After that it runs every weekday morning.
- **You get:** blockers (with a draft ready), what's due or late, what people are waiting on from you, and today's meetings.
- **You do:** read it. Reply by number if you want: "1 done", "2 waiting on Khalid".
- **Good to know:** it reads only your own email, calendar, and tickets. It sends nothing except this email to you.
