# email-draft
 
Writes a new email with a clear ask, in your voice.
 
- **Start it:** say "draft an email" and say why you're sending it. Paste any notes, tickets, or threads.
- **You get:** two or three subject lines, the draft, and a list of anything it assumed.
- **You do:** check the assumptions, edit, and send it yourself.
- **Good to know:** the first time, it asks for two or three emails you wrote, to learn your voice. It never sends anything.
