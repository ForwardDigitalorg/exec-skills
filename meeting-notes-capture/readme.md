# meeting-notes-capture
 
For meetings with no AI note-taker.
 
- **Start it:** say "capture my meeting notes" and paste whatever you have: a transcript, rough notes, the call chat, or a few lines from memory.
- **You get:** a clear record of requests, next steps, dates, and decisions. Then two versions to paste: one for the ticket, one for the team wiki.
- **You do:** answer two or three questions, then approve.
- **Tip:** use it right after the meeting, while it's fresh.
